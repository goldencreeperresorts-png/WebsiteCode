House rules
for a peaceful stay

A few simple rules help us look after the villa, our neighbours and every guest who stays with us.

Up to 12 guests

The villa hosts a maximum of 12 guests. Only the guests named in the booking may stay.

No parties or events

Parties and large gatherings of any kind are strictly prohibited.

Quiet after 10 PM

Please keep music and voices low after 10 PM out of respect for our neighbours.

No smoking indoors

Smoking is allowed only on the balconies and terraces. Please use the ashtrays provided.

No pets

We're unable to host pets at the property.

Valid ID at check-in

Every guest completes a registration form with a driving licence, Aadhaar, voter ID or passport. Foreign nationals also share passport and visa details.

Children & balconies

Please supervise children at all times and don't lean on the glass railings.

Comfort & power

Room heaters are available on request. During power cuts, lights and sockets on the inverter keep running.

General regulations

  • Guests must not pursue any commercial or illegal activity on the property, including using the host's internet connection to access illegal sites.
  • Guests must not take anything from the property that belongs to the owner.
  • Guests are responsible for the safe custody of all valuables and money they bring. The housekeeper and manager are not responsible for any loss or theft.
  • Please turn off gas, stoves, switches and appliances when not in use. Guests are liable for any damage caused by negligence.
  • Please hand over the house keys to the caretaker at check-out.

Security

  • Whenever you leave the villa, please make sure all windows and doors are closed and locked to keep the villa secure and to keep out rain, insects, mosquitoes and animals.
  • The owner and caretaker are not responsible for any accident or self-harm during the stay.

Footwear

  • Please leave your footwear at the villa entrance and use the slippers provided inside.

Noise & neighbourhood

  • Guests are fully responsible for the safety and security of their children at all times.
  • Music and volume must be kept to the lowest level after 10 PM. Neighbours may complain to the local authorities about loud noise, and guests will bear any resulting charges.

Visitors

  • Only the number of guests booked may stay; extra guests are not allowed. The property has a maximum of 12 guests, and bookings that exceed this will not be honoured.

Events

  • Parties and large gatherings of any kind are strictly prohibited.
  • A cleaning charge of Rs 1,000 applies for extra cleaning, for example after a party.

Pets

  • Pets are not allowed on the property.

Balcony & deck areas

  • Do not lean on the balcony glass railings. Babies and children must be supervised on the balconies at all times.
  • Clothes may be dried only on the drying racks, not on the balcony or terrace railings.

Power & backup

  • Electric room heaters are provided on request.
  • During power cuts, only the lights and sockets on the inverter connection will work, on the in-house UPS.

Smoking

  • No smoking inside the property. Smoking is allowed only on the balcony and terrace areas.
  • No chewing tobacco or spitting in or around the property. Cigarette butts must go in the ashtrays provided, not on the grounds. Any damage will be charged to the guest.

Damages & breakages

  • Please do not move or rearrange any furniture, artefacts, cutlery, linen or decorations.
  • Guests and their party are responsible for any damage to or loss of these items.

Compliance

  • Breaking any of these house rules breaches the terms and conditions of occupancy. The owner and manager may end the stay of any guest or visitor who refuses to follow them.
