Golden Creeper, a living journey

From the moment you arrive, you are welcomed by the hush of the forest and the scent of jasmine on warm air. Winding paths lead you through gardens where golden vines climb towards the light.

What makes Golden Creeper
so unique in their eyes?

An art of living inherited from the land itself. Mornings begin with birdsong and fresh fruit from our orchards; evenings end with lantern light reflected in still water.

Everything here grows slowly, beautifully and with intention, just like the golden creeper that gives us our name.

Contemplate and fall under its spell:
Golden Creeper grows with every season

“In every walk with nature one receives far more than he seeks.”

Golden Creeper has always had a gift for renewal. With each season the gardens change their colours, our chefs reinvent their menus and new experiences take root, yet the spirit of the place remains untouched.

Lounges open onto the forest, a library glows by firelight and the infinity pool seems to pour straight into the valley. An invitation to dream, every single day.
